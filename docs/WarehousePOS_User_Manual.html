01 System Overview & Local Directories

WarehousePOS is designed as a high-performance, single-PC offline Windows application built on C# (.NET 10), WPF, and Entity Framework Core with SQLite. It powers all retail transactions, warehouse inventory operations, and dot-matrix continuous invoice printing for Happy Products without requiring cloud servers or active internet connections.

1.1 Operating Environment & Storage Paths

Resource Local Windows Path Function & Security
Live Database C:\ProgramData\WarehousePOS\Data\WarehousePOS.db Permanent operational store (WAL mode enabled).
Automatic Backups C:\ProgramData\WarehousePOS\Backups\ Daily snapshots taken automatically on launch.
System Logs C:\ProgramData\WarehousePOS\Logs\application.log Serilog structured logs (30-day retention).
Application Binaries C:\Program Files\WarehousePOS\WarehousePOS.Desktop.exe 64-bit self-contained desktop binary.

02 User Authentication (Actual System Screen)

System access is secured using individual credentials. Passwords are never stored in plain text; all authentication utilizes salted BCrypt hashing.

Figure 2.1: Actual Happy Products Sign-In Window displaying entry of sample demonstration credentials.

2.1 How to Sign In with Sample Data

1
Enter Username: Click into the Username text box. Type the assigned handle. For demonstration and training, use sample account: HappyProducts (or cashier accounts cashier1, cashier2).
2
Enter Password: Click into the Password box. The input displays security bullets (e.g., ••••••••••). Use the eye icon toggle button on the right edge to briefly verify input if needed.
3
Sign In: Click the green Sign In button or press Enter. The authentication event is recorded in the AuditLog and the system transitions directly into the main shell.

04 Point of Sale (Cash) Terminal

Navigation
Click 🖥️ Point of Sale on the sidebar, or press F1 from any screen.
Figure 4.1: Actual Point of Sale (Cash) Terminal showing live catalog, active price tiers, customer input, and billing panel.

4.1 On-Screen Controls & Checkout Workflow

  1. Product Search Bar: Type product name, SKU (e.g., CFDL72364), or scan the barcode. Items appear in the table with stock counts and prices.
  2. Catalog Quick Add: Click the green + Add button on any item row to add 1 unit to the cart.
  3. Customer Assignment:
    • For counter sales, leave as Walk-in Customer.
    • To record a client name/phone, type in Walk-in Name and Phone.
    • Check Save to Customer Directory to automatically register them for credit tracking.
  4. Price Tier Selection: Switch between Retail Price and Wholesale Price using the radio buttons.
  5. Concessions & Surcharges: Enter optional Discount (Rs.), Delivery Fee (Rs.), or Labour Cost (Rs.). The TOTAL updates dynamically.
  6. Payment Settlement: Select Cash, enter Amount Paid (Rs.). The exact change calculates instantly. Click Complete Sale (or press F12) to finalize the transaction, record inventory decrement, and trigger the Epson LQ-310 printer.

05 Bill History & Invoice Inquiries

Navigation
Click 🧾 Bill History on the left navigation sidebar.
Figure 5.1: Actual Bill History & Invoices screen displaying historical invoices, totals, statuses, and search filters.

5.1 On-Screen Features & Actions

  • Summary Metrics: Shows Total Invoices, Total Paid / Collected (Rs.), and Total Outstanding / Credit (Rs.) for the selected date window.
  • Search & Filter Bar: Filter by invoice number, customer phone number, date range (From / To), Status (Completed, Returned, Layaway), and Payment Method. Click Search to query.
  • Invoice Details Viewer: Click any invoice row in the table to display the itemized breakdown on the right pane, inspect discounts, verify cashier name, and click Reprint Receipt to reissue physical bills.
  • Returns & Adjustments: Select an eligible invoice to process customer product returns or adjust delivery expenses.

07 Product Catalog & Inventory Management

Navigation
Click 📦 Products on the left navigation sidebar.
Figure 7.1: Actual Product Catalog screen displaying master inventory records, stock levels, categories, and actions.

7.1 On-Screen Actions & Workflows

  • Add New Product: Click the green + Add Product button in the top right to open the product registration form (SKU, barcode, category, cost price, retail price, wholesale price, and reorder levels).
  • Manage Categories: Click Manage Categories to create and organize product categories (e.g., MATTRESS, Home Appliances, Electronics).
  • Bulk CSV Import: Click Import CSV to batch-upload hundreds of product records from a spreadsheet.
  • Stock Level Tracking: The Stock column highlights available physical units and displays low-stock indicators in red text.
  • Low-Stock Products Alert: The bottom banner shows current low-stock count and includes a Show Low-Stock Only toggle switch for rapid reordering.
  • Editing & Deactivation: Use Edit to modify prices or specifications; use Deactivate for safe soft-deletion without breaking historical invoice data.

08 Purchasing & Stock Inbound (GRN)

Navigation
Click 🛒 Purchasing on the left navigation sidebar (Admin / Manager role required).
Figure 8.1: Actual Purchasing & Stock Inbound view for processing supplier purchase orders, discounts, and GRN receipts.

8.1 Procurement & Goods Receiving Workflow

  1. Supplier & Terms: Select the vendor from the Select Supplier dropdown (e.g., Arpico, Piyestra Furniture). Choose payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Credit).
  2. Invoice Reference: Type supplier delivery note or invoice number in Purchase Order Notes / Invoice No.
  3. Add Purchase Items: Select product, input Paid Qty, Free Qty (bonus units), Unit Cost (Rs.), and Discount Rate (%). Total Cost calculates automatically.
  4. Payment Recording: Input Paid Amount (Rs.). The system computes Remaining Balance and updates the vendor's payable ledger.
  5. Stock Inbound: Click the green Save & Receive Stock button. The inventory increments atomically via PurchaseReceive movements, and goods are immediately available at the POS terminal.

09 Supplier Directory & Accounts Payable

Navigation
Click 🚚 Suppliers on the left navigation sidebar.
Figure 9.1: Actual Suppliers screen displaying vendor contacts, outstanding payable balances, and benefit actions.

9.1 Managing Vendors & Balances

  • Master Directory: Displays Supplier Name (e.g., Arpico, Piyestra Furniture), Contact Person, Phone number, and current Balance (Rs.) owed to the supplier.
  • Adding Vendors: Click the green + Add Supplier button to register a new vendor company, representative, and address.
  • Supplier Benefits & Entitlements: Click Benefits on any supplier row to view contractual free items, special trade discounts, or rebate agreements.
  • Import CSV: Batch import supplier lists using the Import CSV tool.
  • Settling Accounts: When payments are made by cheque or bank transfer, record payment entries to decrement the supplier's outstanding balance.

Navigation

13.1 Managing Staff Accounts & Permissions

  • Employee Directory: Lists all staff accounts (e.g., happyproducts, cashier1, cashier2), showing assigned Role (Admin, Cashier, Worker), Base Salary (Rs.), Payment Due Date, Status (Active), and Deactivate action.

    3. Select Role (Worker, Admin).

15 Daily Checklists & Shortcut Hotkeys

15.1 Daily Cashier Opening Checklist

1
Boot Workstation: Power on host PC. Verify system date and time are accurate.
2
Power Epson LQ-310: Verify green Ready LED is illuminated. Ensure continuous 2-ply tractor paper is aligned at perforation line.
3
Launch POS & Login: Open WarehousePOS, input individual credentials, and sign in.
4
Count Cash Float: Verify opening cash drawer balance matches opening register records.

15.2 Daily Store Closing Checklist

1
Reconcile Daily Register: Navigate to Reports ➔ Today's Live Sales. Balance cash and card slips against cash drawer contents.
2
Print Closing Summary: Click Print to Epson LQ-310 to generate the signed daily audit slip.
3
Trigger Database Backup: Navigate to Settings ➔ Database Backup, click Backup Database Now. Copy to external USB drive weekly.
4
Logout & Shutdown: Click Logout on the sidebar and shut down Windows cleanly.
